What the numbers show

01

Digital Services (Jio) EBITDA margin rose from 50.1% to 51.1% across three years, while its revenue grew 32% — the group's most consistent profit engine.

02

Oil to Chemicals (O2C) margin fell from 11.0% to 9.1% over the same period, pulled down by weaker transportation-fuel cracks and softer downstream chemical spreads — a reminder that revenue growth and margin health don't always move together.

03

EPS dropped from ₹102.9 to ₹51.5 between FY2023-24 and FY2024-25 — not because profit fell (it grew 2.9%), but because Reliance issued a 1:1 bonus share, doubling the share count. Reading a ratio without reading its denominator is a common analysis mistake.

04

Media (Network18/JioStar) is the fastest-growing segment by percentage — EBITDA margin nearly doubled from 7.5% to 16.7% in two years, though it remains the smallest business by absolute size.
